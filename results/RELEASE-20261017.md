## Interpretation boundaries

1. A trajectory's geometry screen measures its recorded geometry. Passing a
   short screen does not establish accurate forces or long-term stability.
2. A DFT single-point comparison measures force agreement at that geometry.
   It is not a DFT trajectory or evidence of an adsorption/mineralization event.
3. A training-fit threshold applies to the frames used in optimization. It is
   not independent validation and cannot qualify a model for a larger system.
4. Replicating a periodic cell preserves the intended coverage only when both
   substrate and adsorbates are replicated consistently. It does not establish
   slab-thickness, lateral-size, vacuum, or coverage convergence.

## Reproduce the report

Follow each package's `REPRODUCE.md`. Bundled numerical tables and portable
plotting scripts support report/figure replay without running CP2K, LAMMPS,
or a training job. Source identities and transformations are recorded alongside
the packages. The release manifest covers all files in the four new result
packages; overview and research-state changes are identified by the Git commit.

Scientific reruns require the exact external assets listed in each package:
for example, full native output, model/checkpoint binaries, initial data, and
the recorded software environment. These assets are not silently replaced
with approximations or synthetic data. A report replay is not a complete
scientific rerun.

## Publication scope

Included: compact numerical results, small relevant scientific inputs and
snapshots, diagnostic figures, portable plotting/checking code, source hashes,
run parameters, and English instructions with negative outcomes preserved.

Excluded: manuscript/paper files; model and checkpoint binaries; large raw
trajectories and generated training datasets; wavefunctions; sealed validation
labels; private host paths; credentials; approval and scheduler records;
client/GPU operational diagnostics; unrelated omniMD materials; and unlicensed
third-party documents or datasets. Existing tracked manuscript files are not
modified by this results-only release.
