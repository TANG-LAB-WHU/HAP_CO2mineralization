# Development diagnostic figure captions

Both figures are generated by `../make_figures.py` from the measured tables in `../data/` and `../summary.json`. Original-source identities are retained in `../sources.json`; package hashes are in `../manifest.json`. No synthetic values, uncertainty bars, resampling or model evaluation are used.

**force-gates.png.** A: measured force-component RMSE for 90 training frames, 16 R9/R10 development frames and the single 10-fs DFT endpoint, alongside the unchanged original limits. B: the endpoint CO2 force RMSE exceeds its separately frozen 0.05 eV/angstrom exploratory limit; that endpoint limit does not qualify the earlier development set. C: Ca/P/H endpoint errors also exceed the original element limit. These populations differ and do not form a controlled before/after comparison. The uncomputed 20-fs DFT result is absent, never imputed as zero.

**nve-diagnostic.png.** Signed total-energy departure and instantaneous temperature for one seed and 81 correlated frames over 20 fs. The red line locates the only DFT-checked endpoint at 10 fs; its CO2 force screen failed. Small energy departure and intact geometry establish neither DFT force accuracy nor equilibration. The 1 meV/atom original energy-departure limit lies outside the plotted zoom.

All results remain development diagnostics with `manuscript_eligible=false` and `production_released=false`.
