# Model, settings and interpretation

The common fixed triclinic cell has rows (9.424,0,0), (-4.712,8.161423405265,0), (0,0,37.7401146) angstrom, with XYZ periodic boundaries. A and B contain the same 88-atom dry parallel-OH HAP substrate and one CO2 (91 atoms, 592 valence electrons); clean88 has 576 electrons and gas3 has 16. Neutral closed-shell calculations use PBE-D3(BJ), 1200/80 Ry grid cutoffs, DZVP-MOLOPT-SR-GTH/GTH-PBE, EPS_SCF 1e-8 and the same Z dipole correction. All atoms move; cell and method remain fixed.

A reuses the prior parallel-OH reference geometry and WFN as starting conditions, then receives the stricter common refinement. B changes only initial CO2 position/orientation: its carbon starts 2.70 angstrom above surface phosphate O78, bonded to P58, and the molecular axis is along x. This predetermined starting arrangement is not a site ranking. Exact structures, mapping and construction are retained in the inputs and geometry-selection record.

The four simultaneous optimization limits are maximum force component 0.010 eV/angstrom, RMS force components 0.005 eV/angstrom, maximum displacement 0.003 bohr and RMS displacement 0.0015 bohr. The exact atomic-unit values and BFGS trust radius 0.05 angstrom are in method.json and the inputs. Initial optimizations use fresh BFGS history; B continuations explicitly reuse their matching native WFN and approximate Hessian, with repeated evaluations charged. No threshold relaxation accompanies a continuation.

The electronic energy convention is Eads(A)=E(A91,relaxed)-E(clean88,relaxed)-E(gas3,relaxed), using 27.211386245988 eV/Hartree. A/clean/gas terminal energy-only SCF values are used in this subtraction. They must not be attached to stale force frames: energy-only final records are explicitly excluded from force labels. The force table identifies the last complete force evaluation separately.

The official pinned CUDA CP2K image, executable/data hashes and resource limits are in method.json. The recorded execution uses one MPI rank, eight OpenMP threads, 24 GiB RAM without swap and one GPU. These execution checks establish bounded output integrity, not method convergence, isolated-reference convergence, a global minimum, finite-temperature free energy or mineralization chemistry. B never reached the four criteria, so there is no A/B ranking and no interaction/deformation decomposition.
