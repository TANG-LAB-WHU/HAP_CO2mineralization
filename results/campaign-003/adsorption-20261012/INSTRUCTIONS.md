# Use and release instructions

- Read status, method and limitations before interpreting any energy. Report A's value as conditional; never use B's partial endpoint for an A/B preference.
- Keep source hashes and units attached to derived tables. Preserve original forces; do not subtract net force or fit an energy offset.
- Distinguish terminal energy-only evaluations from complete geometry/force pairs. Do not insert zeros for incomplete evaluations or missing results.
- Keep R4 sealed. Do not open or hash its numerical files as part of reporting.
- Run only the report verifier to check this package. Scientific reruns require the matching omitted assets and a separately bounded reviewed execution plan; historical commands are not an automatic launch queue.
- Keep raw trajectories, WFN, Hessians and operational logs in their original retention locations. The manifest does not imply those assets were committed to Git.
- Keep document sources/build products, credentials, private paths and internal communication out of this result package.
