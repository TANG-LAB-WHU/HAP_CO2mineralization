\subsection{Proposed targeted sampling campaign: not executed}
The next campaign is a prospectively specified plan, not additional completed work.
It remains restricted to the same dry 47-atom cell. Six single points would first test
900/80 versus 1100/100 Ry force-grid sensitivity on three distinct geometries, retaining
the 0.02 eV/\AA{} numerical gate. The proposed initial design contains 48 training
configurations balanced across hydroxyl restoring-force probes, phosphate/calcium
distortions, and molecular/interface configurations. Separately seeded parent families
provide 24 validation and 24 sealed test configurations. Coordinate hashes and roles
would be fixed before DFT labeling; the existing 0.025 \AA{} cross-split distance-vector
criterion would be retained. This does not establish thermal statistical independence.

Four fixed training seeds would use the existing wider architecture. At most two
acquisition rounds would add 24 training labels each, selected from development
configurations using diversity, structural excursions and a predeclared 0.15 eV/\AA{}
committee force-disagreement trigger. Such disagreement is a heuristic, not guaranteed
error detection. The total budget is 159 new DFT attempts, including the six grid
checks, 96 initial labels, 48 possible acquisitions and nine preselected dynamical
spot checks. Failed calculations consume slots; no open-ended extra round is authorized.

The existing gates remain fixed: one-frame force RMSE below 0.02 eV/\AA, new sealed-test
force RMSE below 0.10 eV/\AA{} and energy RMSE below 10 meV/atom without a test-fitted
offset; same-state engine and strict restart tolerances are unchanged. Three new
velocity seeds would be examined at both 0.25 and 0.125 fs over identical 1 ps durations,
with extension to 5 ps only after development gates pass. All saved frames must satisfy
the original geometry bounds and absolute NVE drift must remain below 5 meV/atom/ps.
The nine DFT checks are fixed at 0, 0.5 and 1 ps for the three half-step trajectories.

The new sealed test would be opened once after model selection. Any failure ends that
campaign without changing a threshold; further development requires a new plan and
untouched holdout. The present 0.10788 eV/\AA{} result is retrospective/exploratory and
cannot serve that role. The bundled future-work specification records seeds, counts,
distortion ranges and stopping rules. No new sampling or HPC submission is executed
by this proposal, and even success would not establish production mineralization.
