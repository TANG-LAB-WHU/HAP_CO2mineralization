Do not claim the first atomistic HAP-CO2 study. Separate molecular adsorption, carbonate
formation/incorporation, and persistent mineral storage. A favorable electronic adsorption
energy addresses neither reaction kinetics nor durable storage. Water, termination, Ca/P,
protonation, OH orientation and charge must be stated. Conditions from different papers
cannot be transferred to local predictions. Literature does not repair failed validation.
