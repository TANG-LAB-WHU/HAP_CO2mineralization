This short duration cannot establish long-term stability, and it does not override independent-force errors.

The actual NVE temperature changes from 200.0 K to 1853.1 K, with a maximum of 4079.8 K. This temperature evolution accompanies changes in the learned potential energy; it is not evidence of a validated reaction or thermal ensemble. The trajectory geometry gate is \textbf{failed}, with 43 of 101 saved frames failing the stated contact/PO4/OH criteria. Energy conservation cannot override these structural and predictive-accuracy limits.

At the same 250 fs endpoint, halving the timestep changes unwrapped positions by an atom-wise RMS of 1.8834 \AA{} and velocities by at most 246.61 \AA/ps per component. These measured differences supplement the two drift slopes; no post hoc coordinate-convergence threshold is imposed.

A separate zero-step binary-restart load preserves the saved boundary to within 2.85e-13 \AA{} in positions and 5.77e-11 \AA/ps in velocity components relative to the separately executed continuous trajectory. Tiny thermo differences already occur before the restart. These observations are compatible with numerical roundoff/order sensitivity; they do not establish a unique cause, and the strict 250 fs endpoint gate remains failed. The half-timestep endpoint minimum-image position RMS difference is 0.8603 \AA. Passing the drift-slope criterion therefore does not establish trajectory agreement or timestep convergence.
